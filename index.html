    Sources: problem and solution

    01

    Memory against a device

    Over 30 days, a Bluetooth smart e‑cigarette logged 34.85 puffs a day against 15.56 self-reported for the same days: about 2.2 times more.

    Li Z, Benowitz-Fredericks C, Ling PM, Cohen JE, Thrul J. Nicotine & Tobacco Research 2021;23(5):842-848. 18 young adults aged 21 to 26, US, 2017. Small study; the device was not their usual vape.doi.org/10.1093/ntr/ntaa205
    02

    Memory, the other way

    In a 4-hour video-observed session, people tended to over-report, and their usual puffs a day matched observed puffs only weakly (r = 0.43, where 1 is a perfect match).

    Dowd AN, et al. Nicotine & Tobacco Research 2023. 57 adult daily vapers, Buffalo, US, 2020 to 2021. Small lab study.doi.org/10.1093/ntr/ntad037
    03

    No sessions to count

    Asked how many separate times a day they vape, 49.0% (1,418 of 2,896) chose “I vape continuously throughout the day”, the most common answer.

    Martinez U, et al. Nicotine & Tobacco Research 2020;22(5):764-770. 2,896 US adults who smoke and vape weekly; online recruitment, Jul 2016 to Jul 2017. Not a random sample.doi.org/10.1093/ntr/ntz043
    04

    Vaping in Spain

    5% of people aged 15 to 64 used an e‑cigarette in the last 30 days in 2024, up from 2% in 2018.

    OEDA, EDADES 2018 to 2024, e-cigarette infographic (Ministerio de Sanidad). EDADES 2024: 26,878 people interviewed at home, Feb to Jun 2024.pnsd.sanidad.gob.es
    05

    Monitoring progress

    Across 138 randomised studies (19,951 people; mostly weight, activity and blood pressure, one on smoking), prompting people to monitor their progress improved goal attainment (effect size 0.40: small to medium).

    Harkin B, Webb TL, Chang BP, et al. Psychological Bulletin 2016;142(2):198-229. Not tested on vaping.doi.org/10.1037/bul0000025
    06

    Puff length and nicotine

    Machine-made aerosol from one e‑cigarette: across puff profiles, power and liquid strength (18 to 36 mg/ml), the nicotine from 15 puffs ranged more than 50‑fold. Longer puffs gave more nicotine.

    Talih S, Balhas Z, Eissenberg T, et al. Nicotine & Tobacco Research 2015;17(2):150-157, online 2014. A lab machine study, not people.doi.org/10.1093/ntr/ntu174

    Which rules apply

    • E-cigarette product rules: no, while the clip stays outside the vape’s air path, is not needed to vape and is sold on its own. Built into a mouthpiece or tank, it would become a vape part.
    • Radio and electronics: yes, before any sale, free pilot units included: CE marking, RoHS and e-waste registration.
    • Data protection: yes. Puff data is likely health data. Today it stays on the phone: no server, no account.
    • Vape advertising bans: yes, now. They cover any message that promotes vaping, whoever sends it. We never promote vaping.
    • Medical devices: no, as long as we make no treatment claims. Wane is a lifestyle tool, not a treatment.
    • Spain’s tobacco bill (2026): not law yet. Its broad “related product” definition could catch accessories. We watch it.
    TPD 2014/40/EU art. 2(16) and 20(5); Radio Equipment Directive 2014/53/EU; GDPR art. 9; Spain Ley 28/2005; bill 121/000110 (BOCG, 11 Sep 2026). Our research summary of 5 Oct 2026, checked by a reviewer. Not legal advice.

    What people use today

    • Memory. Without a tool, the only record is memory.
    • Tap-to-log apps (Puff Count, Puffless, Vaporate). Each puff is tapped by hand; they cannot see puff length.
    • Puff counters on some pod vapes. They count automatically, but only on that device, and show no length.
    • The JUUL2 app. Tracks use over time, but only on JUUL’s own pods, and it is run by the company that sells the nicotine.
    • PuffPacket: a Cornell research device (2020) that attaches to e‑cigarettes and records puffs. Open designs; not a product.
    • Quit apps (Smoke Free, Kwit, QuitNow). Built for smokers; they don’t measure vaping automatically.

    Puff counting itself is not new. Our part: each puff’s time and length, tracked automatically on your own refillable vape, by a company that sells no nicotine.

    App store and product pages, 3 Oct 2026. Juul Labs, submission to the UK CMA, Feb 2025. PuffPacket: Adams et al., Cornell Tech, ACM CHI 2020.

    Product boundaries and safeguards

    • Adults only, permanently. Setup starts with “I’m 18 or older and I vape”. Marketing aimed at 25 and over.
    • Nothing for teens. No games or points for puffing, no gadget look, no youth channels. We never promote vaping, and Wane never sells nicotine.
    • Your data stays on your phone. No account needed.
    • The coach (year 2) never raises nicotine. Pregnancy (or trying to be) or breastfeeding stops the plan.
